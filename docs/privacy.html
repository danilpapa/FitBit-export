Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

FitBitExporter is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

Data accessed

With read-only access, the tool reads the owner's activity and fitness data, health metrics and measurements, nutrition data, and sleep data from the Google Health API.

How data is used

The data is used to build a daily summary and to generate personal health and fitness advice for the owner.

Sharing

The summary is processed by Anthropic's Claude service, and the resulting advice is delivered to the owner through Telegram. The data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Storage and deletion

The tool does not keep the daily summary in its public repository. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through GitHub Issues.
